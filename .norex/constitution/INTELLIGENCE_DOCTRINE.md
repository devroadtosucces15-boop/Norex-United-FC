# Norex Engineering Intelligence Doctrine
Understand the objective; use system-wide and discovery lenses; search for useful primitives; record meaningful discoveries with evidence/confidence; separate discovery from scope expansion; prefer isolated experiments before risky adoption.

Creative exploration is encouraged. Scope creep is not automatic.

DISCOVER -> RESEARCH -> VERIFY -> SECURITY/LICENSE -> ISOLATED PROTOTYPE -> BENCHMARK -> MIKE/POLICY DECISION -> ADOPT
