# Product Vision
Norex engineering should feel simpler than the systems underneath it.

- conversation-first orchestration
- persistent shared engineering intelligence
- provider independence
- local ownership where practical
- evidence-backed work and deterministic validation
- adopt trustworthy existing primitives before rebuilding
- automation without surrendering human authority
- Web/Desktop, Web/Mobile, iOS, and Android are distinct surfaces
- Norex United never depends on Norex Dev OS at runtime

Agents are replaceable. Knowledge is persistent. Capabilities are abstract. State belongs to the system. Decisions belong to Mike.
